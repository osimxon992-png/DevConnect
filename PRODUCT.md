# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Working developers worldwide (not tied to a region). They come to share what they build, ask and answer programming questions, find teammates for side projects, chat, and browse developer jobs. Typical session: check the feed and notifications, answer a question, reply to a message, look at a project's join requests.

## Product Purpose
DevConnect is a social and collaboration network only for developers: profiles with skills, posts with code snippets, a Q&A section with votes and accepted answers, projects that recruit by role and get a team chat, groups, real-time private messages, notifications, jobs, saved items, reports and an admin panel. Success means developers find help and teammates, and come back daily.

## Positioning
One place that joins the social feed, the Q&A, and project recruiting: a question can lead to a follow, a follow to a project, a project to a team chat.

## Operating Context
React (Vite, JSX) SPA on Supabase (auth, Postgres with RLS, storage, realtime). Used on desktop, tablet and phone. Content is user-generated: code, images, short posts, long question bodies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- No AI features inside the product, no AI APIs.
- Everything shown must be backed by the database; no fake data or demo content.
- Interface language: English.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: DevConnect.
- The owner explicitly rejected the earlier look as "obviously AI-made": no gradients anywhere, no purple/violet "AI" palette, no glow, no generic modern-AI-template styling. It must read as made by a human designer.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, user counts, logos, or press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Content first: code and text written by developers are the main thing on every screen.
- Dense but calm: people scan feeds and lists quickly; chrome should not compete.
- Every control does something real.
